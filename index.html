Methodology and limitations

What this metric measures

The metric is annualized temple endowment sessions per member of record. For each geography and date, the seven-day endowment total is multiplied by 365/7 and divided by the geography's membership count.

Rate = (trailing 7-day endowment sessions × 365 ÷ 7) ÷ members of record.

This is a rate of recorded sessions relative to membership, not a count or estimate of unique people attending. One person may attend multiple sessions, and the source data does not identify unique attendees.

Rolling window and annualization

The trailing seven-day window smooths day-to-day variation. Annualization expresses the recent seven-day pace as a yearly rate; it is not a forecast that activity will continue at that level for a full year. The current date is excluded because its activity data may be incomplete.

Comparing multiple geographies

For a selected group, the combined rate is calculated as the sum of annualized endowment sessions divided by the sum of membership counts. It is not the simple average of state or country rates. The aggregate trend is shown only for dates when every selected geography has data, so the group denominator remains consistent over the plotted period.

Sources and caveats

Temple activity counts are sourced from Fuller Consideration's endowment history. Membership denominators come from Church membership statistics gathered by the Temple Stats Python pipeline. Geography is based on each temple's physical location, with documented location overrides where needed.

Membership figures are not necessarily synchronized to the daily activity data, and published membership counts may be rounded or updated on a different schedule. Geographic comparisons should be interpreted as approximate rates, not direct measures of individual participation or religious commitment.
