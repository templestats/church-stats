Methodology and Limitations

What This Metric Measures

This metric estimates the annualized number of temple endowment sessions per member of record. For each geography and date, the trailing seven-day endowment total is multiplied by 365/7 and divided by the membership count.

Rate = (Trailing 7-day endowment sessions × 365 ÷ 7) ÷ Members of Record

This is a rate of recorded sessions relative to membership, not a count or estimate of unique people attending. An individual may attend multiple sessions, and the source data does not identify unique attendees.

Rolling Window and Annualization

The trailing seven-day window smooths day-to-day variation. Annualization expresses the recent seven-day pace as a yearly rate; it does not forecast that activity will continue at that level for a full year. The current date is excluded because its activity data may be incomplete.

Comparing Multiple Geographies

For a selected group, the combined rate is calculated by summing annualized endowment sessions across the selected geographies and dividing by their combined membership count. It is not the simple average of state or country rates.

The aggregate trend is displayed only for dates when every selected geography has data, keeping the group denominator consistent across the plotted period.

Sources and Limitations

Temple activity counts are sourced from Fuller Consideration's endowment history. Membership denominators come from Church membership statistics for the most recently reported year. Geography is based on each temple's physical location.

Geographic comparisons should be interpreted as approximate rates, not direct measures of individual participation or religious commitment. Rates may be affected by people traveling from outside a geography to attend a temple. For example, Illinois's rate may be inflated by non-Illinois residents attending the Nauvoo temple.
